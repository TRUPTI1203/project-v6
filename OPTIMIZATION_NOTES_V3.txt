V3 fixes:
1. AttendancePage now initializes _poll_job before destroy can be called.
2. UI polling is rescheduled continuously every UI_REFRESH_MS. V2 only scheduled poll_ui once.

5. Camera/background-thread optimizations from V2 are preserved.
